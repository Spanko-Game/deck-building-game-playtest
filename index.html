A COOPERATIVE DECK-BUILDING GAME

Spanko

Work together to play Discipline cards, remove each Bottom’s clothing layer by layer, and Punish four Bottoms before the 20-card Supply Closet runs out. Build stronger decks, manage Position, and make every Discipline Action count.

1.Take exactly 1 face-up Supply Closet card each turn.
2.Use up to 1 Utility Action and your available Discipline Actions.
3.Use Discipline cards to punish Bottoms, removing each clothing layer until their bare bottom is thoroughly spanked and their Composure is gone.
4.Punish all 4 Bottoms before the Supply Closet is exhausted.

How to Play
Objective: Cooperate to Punish 4 Bottoms before the 20-card Supply Closet runs out.
Turn structure

At the start of the turn, refill empty face-up Supply Closet slots to the current display limit.

You normally have 1 Utility Action and 3 Discipline Actions. After the third Bottom is Punished, you have 4 Discipline Actions.

During every turn you must take exactly 1 face-up Supply Closet card. Taking it does not use an action.

Utility Actions

You may use up to one built-in Utility Action each turn. These are not cards.

Focus: Draw 1 card, then discard 1 card.

Refine: Permanently remove 1 card from your discard pile.

Repair: Repair 1 Broken Implement and put it into your discard pile.

Action-cost exceptions: Follow Through itself does not use a Discipline Action. One More Time uses its normal Discipline Action, but the card it plays from your discard pile costs 0 additional Discipline Actions.

Discipline Cards & Layers

Every playable card is a Discipline Card. Playing one normally uses 1 Discipline Action.

A Bottom has three Layers in order: Clothing → Underwear → Bare. Impact reduces the current Layer's Composure. Excess Impact carries into the next Layer.

When you break at least one Layer, you normally cannot take any more Discipline Actions that turn. A remaining Utility Action may still be used.

Position

A Bottom may be OTK, Bent Over, or unpositioned. Position is shared by all players and remains between turns until consumed or changed.

A Position is consumed after a Discipline receives a benefit from it, unless the resolving card says otherwise. An effect that only establishes or changes Position does not consume it.

Swat and Smack each gain +1 Impact while the Bottom is OTK or Bent Over, but they do not consume Position.

Implements

Implements have listed Impact, a Favored Position, and a Position Bonus. If the Bottom is in the Favored Position, add the Position Bonus.

Implements normally Break after use and go to the shared Broken Pile. The standard Repair Utility Action moves a repaired Implement to your discard pile.

Progression

Start with 2 face-up Supply Closet cards. After Bottom 1, the display limit becomes 3.

After Bottom 2, normal hand size increases from 4 to 5. After Bottom 3, normal Discipline Actions increase from 3 to 4.

Punish Bottom 4 to win. If the Supply Closet is exhausted before Bottom 4 is Punished, the players lose.
